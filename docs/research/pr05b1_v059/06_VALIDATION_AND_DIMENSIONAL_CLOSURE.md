# Validation and dimensional closure

The local mass diagonal is `(1,0,...,0)` in eta=ln(a). Real/virtual residuals have source rate units before normalization and are algebraic. Causal history arrays are dimensionless occupation departures stored only after a source step is solved. Candidate finite spike masses are proportional to `Delta ln(nu)/x_1s`; because `Delta ln(nu)` is not source-fixed, the mass is not source-identifiable.
