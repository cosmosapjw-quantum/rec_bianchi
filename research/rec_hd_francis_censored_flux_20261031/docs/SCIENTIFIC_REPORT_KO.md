## Derived conditional censored likelihood and convexity
For an aperture and fixed nonnegative model amplitudes `a_k`, let `mu_l = sum_k a_k q_kl` be the nonnegative model HD line flux in the same observed units; `q` comes from original-source non-LTE line power, with numerical row-normalization used solely as an amplitude convention. For **positive printed-error detections** `D`, take independent fixed plug-in errors `sigma_l(eps)=sqrt(s_l^2+eps^2 F_l^2)`, with `eps=0` or `0.06`. For **printed 3σ upper limits** `U`, let the *conditional working* limit noise `sigma_l^(U)=L_l/3`. The conditional likelihood, up to additive parameter-independent Gaussian constants, has deviance

```
D(a; eps) = sum_{l in D} ((F_l - mu_l(a))/sigma_l(eps))^2
            - 2 sum_{l in U} log Phi((L_l - mu_l(a))/(L_l/3)), a_k >= 0,
```

where Phi is the standard-normal CDF and `-log(Phi(z))` is evaluated by stable `scipy.special.log_ndtr`. It is **not** the original JWST joint likelihood, even for eps=.06: both the independence and 3σ-to-noise mapping are explicit assumptions, the printed F-based calibration is a fixed sensitivity scenario, and upper-limit reporting/censoring may be more complex. Inter-aperture correlations are ignored.

Write `z_l=(L_l-mu_l)/(L_l/3)` and the inverse Mills ratio `lambda(z)=phi(z)/Phi(z)`. Then `d[-logPhi(z)]/dz=-lambda(z)` and `d^2[-logPhi(z)]/dz^2=lambda(z)[z+lambda(z)] >= 0`; composing with an affine `mu(a)` yields convexity in the amplitudes for **each fixed model template**, including the boundary `a>=0`. Wolfram Language returned `True, True` for both derivative identities (analytic positivity checked by standard inverse-Mills bounds). Detection terms are also convex. Global optimal amplitude within each template is therefore accessible via NNLS-like constrained convex profiling; this does **not** make the discrete temperature/pressure grid globally smooth nor yield frequentist p-values.
