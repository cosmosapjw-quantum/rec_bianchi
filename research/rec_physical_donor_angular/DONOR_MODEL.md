# REC Physical Directional Donor — Research Model R1

## Core conclusion to test

A physical donor is a causal, reconstructible angular-frequency function with
source and characteristic provenance. It is not a bare vector tied to one
quadrature.

For each typed channel \(c\), use the characteristic representation

\[
\frac{d f_c}{ds}=\eta_c-\chi_c f_c,
\qquad s=ct,
\]

\[
f_c(s,\nu,\Omega)=e^{-\tau_c(s_0,s)}f_c(s_0,\nu_0,\Omega_0)
+\int_{s_0}^{s}e^{-\tau_c(s',s)}\eta_c(s',\nu',\Omega')\,ds'.
\]

The direction and frequency histories \((\Omega',\nu')\) are generated by the
BASS-owned homogeneous photon characteristics and the REC hydrogen-frame
adapter. Atomic emission/absorption data remain REC-owned. This path integral
is the donor generator; any finite grid is a projection of it.

## Two independent information budgets

Following the TEFF seed papers, angular and spectral/fiber resolution are
separate:

\[
f_c(s,\nu,\Omega)
=\sum_{j=0}^{J}\sum_{\ell=0}^{L}\sum_{m=-\ell}^{\ell}
 a^{(c)}_{j\ell m}(s)\,\psi_j(\nu)Y_{\ell m}(\Omega)
+r_c(s,\nu,\Omega).
\]

Increasing \(L\) cannot repair unresolved frequency structure, and increasing
\(J\) cannot repair a narrow angular beam or a half-range discontinuity.

## Survivor architecture

1. **Physical authority:** causal characteristic generator and immutable input provenance.
2. **Low-order backbone:** PSTF/spherical-harmonic coefficients through an adaptively selected \(L_c\), used for rotationally invariant diagnostics and coupling to BASS.
3. **Positive adaptive residual:** nodal values on a hierarchical spherical mesh or interpolatory point set, refined near beams, inflow-mask boundaries, and event-surface changes.
4. **Projection layer:** positive-weight Lebedev sequence, including the current 26-point rule, for integration and regression only.
5. **Realizability:** total occupation is pointwise nonnegative; signed distortion \(\Delta f\) is stored separately. Moment-only reconstructions require a realizability/entropy certificate.
6. **Certificates:** low-order moment conservation, orientation sensitivity, half-range flux convergence, positivity, angular/spectral refinement, and donor-generator identity.

## Status taxonomy

- `SOURCE_IDENTICAL_DIRECTIONAL_DONOR`: only after the causal generator and all inputs are exactly pinned and replayed.
- `CLOSURE_DEFINED_DIRECTIONAL_SURROGATE`: maximum-entropy or minimum-information lift from incomplete moments. Useful as an initial guess or error-control baseline, never source authority.
- `QUADRATURE_PROJECTION`: values of either object on a named grid. The current 26-vector belongs here.
