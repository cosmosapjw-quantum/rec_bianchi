# C2N REC equilibrium-limited exponential extent
Separate from REI high-frequency convergence. On the feasible interval, for strictly positive forward/backward rates A,B, A/B = b/(neutral-b) * (1+ft)/ft * [(1+fc)/fc for two-photon or fc/(1+fc) for Raman]. b decreases with extent d, ft increases via the strictly monotone activity inversion; fc increases for two-photon and decreases for Raman. All three factors decrease (atomic factor strictly). Thus log(A/B) strictly decreases on the interior. If boundary signs bracket a root, unique equilibrium extent d*, and sign(F)=sign(d*-d) for F=A-B. This does not assert monotonic magnitude of F.
Write x along the signed direction, x*=|d*|, G(x)=sign(d*) F(sign(d*)x)>0 on [0,x*), k(x)=G(x)/(x*-x). Assuming smooth finite k and positive stable derivative at equilibrium, a second-order map is x_mid=x*(1-exp[-h k(0)/2]), x_new=x*(1-exp[-h k(x_mid)]). This is exponential midpoint on the transformed log distance to equilibrium; expansion x_new=h G0+h^2 G0 G'(0)/2+O(h^3). It preserves 0<=x_new<=x* and approaches equilibrium for h->infinity when k near x* has positive finite limit. Not a general exact subflow, not a uniform stiffness order proof; finite precision bracket or invalid k rejects. Near saturation sample the rate at x*(1-1e-6) only for the rate evaluation, not to clip the updated extent. The exact single-event solution stays inside equilibrium interval. Stoichiometric invariants remain exact in arithmetic. Gross-event midpoint quadrature is diagnostic, not exact gross reaction integration. Symmetric forward/reverse composition and source/SSPRK2 splitting retain formal second order for smooth non-stiff steps; scatter positivity remains CFL-conditional.
