## Exact theory / code contract
For each source aperture the two author model components have **the same density**, \`n1=n2=10^(logn_bestfit) [cm^-3]\`; unlike isobaric \`n_c=(p/kB)/T_c\`, their component \`p_c/kB=nT_c\` may differ. T1,T2, logN1,logN2, OPR1,OPR2, xHD and tau97 read from published isochoric bestfit source. H2 para/ortho LOGPOP from original FITS is separately normalized, f_J = g_J 10^LOGPOP with bilinear interpolation in logT/logn on the original grid, no extrapolation or NaN replacement. Optical thin \`I_ul = hc * (E_u-E_l)[cm^-1] A_ul Sum_c N_u,c / (4 pi)\`. H2 emitter spin weight is 1/(1+OPR) on even J or OPR/(1+OPR) on odd J. HD N_u includes molecular xHD, never elemental D/H. Original source HD collider mixture unchanged.

Observed-frame working predictions are **conditioned on two measured H2 S(1) and S(2) anchor throughputs**; KP5 fixes spectral shape but cannot disentangle beam filling and physical dust. Gamma 2-anchor parameters and xHD amplitude are profiled with published Gaussian errors and assumed \`sigma=L/3\` on HD upper limits, epsilon=0/0.06 alternative. Eight rounded-zero HD errors excluded; no observed cross-line covariance or independent Bayesian posterior has been recovered.
