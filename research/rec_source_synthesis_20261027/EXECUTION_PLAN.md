## Failure handling and transition

A source/contract mismatch, ambiguous scientific convention, exhausted unit cap or needed model change returns to the parent. Failed output and debit are retained. A concrete current-task defect permits one bounded repair episode under the same frozen physical contract and remaining budget. Success advances to result interpretation and independent review; it does not trigger recursive review.
