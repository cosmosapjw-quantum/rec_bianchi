## First mathematical discriminants

For w=exp(-x), x'=x, direct differentiation gives w''=x(x-1)exp(-x). This is not uniformly concave or convex on the unrestricted positive-energy domain: the sign changes at x=1. A full-cell bound therefore needs stationary points, not endpoint sampling without a regime proof.

Differentiating again gives w'''=-x(x^2-3x+1)exp(-x). The positive stationary points of w'' are r_minus=(3-sqrt(5))/2 and r_plus=(3+sqrt(5))/2. The absolute value additionally has a zero at x=1, which cannot increase a maximum. Thus a finite candidate set yields an exact analytic supremum, with interval-safe inclusion. This directly distinguishes the selected construction from an empirical grid maximum.

Let qstar=-ln((1+z)Es/Et). Then Es*exp(qstar)/(kN*T0)=Et/(kN*T0*(1+z)), proving exact characteristic reference equality. The represented query qhat differs from qstar, while the represented native TRhat also differs from the exact product kN*T0*(1+z). Keeping both bridges separately exposes which representation causes each term.

The selected native Wien model is fixed by the source, rather than chosen by a fit. Replacing it with Planck occupation or a modern SI constant would change the model being connected.
