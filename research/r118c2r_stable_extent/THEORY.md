## Observed failure and repair

Instead create a per-event local trial map at current state: F(delta)=F_current + r[Delta product_A(delta)-Delta product_B(delta)], with tracked scalar activity change solved around current occupation and delta only. No subtraction of two global increments in root evaluations. This preserves midpoint variation in finite precision (baseline error remains). Rerun all actual source-only and coupled cases; C2N roundoff fallback is retained but not used in these tests.
