## Exact-original-method limits and license

The fixed REC recurrence has no exposed banded LU/coupled Newton solve to replace. Same h alone does not make varying atomic/thermal matrices identical. Directly importing the RODAS/homotopy step, GMRES fallback, Krylov projection or changed acceptance criteria would replace the original method and predictor-history contract. PR74/75 also reject nonautonomous problems; REC's continuous EMLA diagnostic explicitly is nonautonomous.

Source inspection found slice/zip loops and fixed-order reductions, not a new explicit SIMD kernel or measured SIMD contribution. PR74's compensated dot product changes arithmetic relative to ordinary sums; it cannot be substituted into original reductions on an exact-output assumption. Original recurrence ordering forbids reassociation/FMA changes. PR73's bit agreement is relative to its upstream Slices implementation, not HyRec.

REC's `ffi.rs` protects original C static rates/cosmology with one process-local mutex. Threading those calls retains serialization; dependent timesteps and stage guards cannot simply be dispatched concurrently. Independent-process scheduling around the unchanged executable and exact-byte cache/contiguous-access patterns remain possible design candidates, with applicability unmeasured here.
