retained7489-row photon history. C is the original313-query SOURCE_CONTEXT.

## State and clocks
Native global row n uses z_n=(1+ZSTART)exp(-n*DLNA)-1 evaluated as native C.
Radiation-local i=n-izH0 and source zH0 define a separate local uniform chart.
Existing H5 stored endpoints a_j,b_j and query q_j are kept exactly; no clock
is silently replaced by -log(1+rounded z). Theta_n denotes committed native
thermodynamic data and phase. C_m denotes an actual source-call record with
its evaluated thermodynamic inputs, derivative return, local slot and ordered
call id. W_i is the final observed writer of photon slot i. A trial call can
have inputs distinct from Theta_(izH0+i). Source photon y_i and these two
thermodynamic views are retained independently.

Hydrogen fractions xe,xHII,xH1s,xHeII are dimensionless. Tm_driver is Kelvin;
TM/TR passed to hydrogen are eV; nH passed to hydrogen is cm^-3; H is s^-1.
Unobserved branch-local fractions are null unless explicitly fixed by that
native closure; a source probe is never called a committed node.
