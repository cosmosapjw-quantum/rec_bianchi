# Validation and dimensional closure

The public recombination coefficient has dimension m^3 s^-1, so n_H alpha has s^-1. Photoionization, R2p2s and integrated native-bin A coefficients have s^-1. RadiationFeedback uses rho_gamma [J m^-3], p_gamma and pi_gamma [Pa], q_gamma [W m^-2], and tetrad Q_atom [W m^-3]. The local scalar sector receives only physical tetrad data through BackgroundSnapshot.
