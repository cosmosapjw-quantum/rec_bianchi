# Orbit-cache conductance formalism

For an active scalar orbit \(\mathcal O=(i,j,\mu)\),

\[
Q_{\mathcal O}={h\over c}
\sqrt{\nu_i^2+\nu_j^2-2\nu_i\nu_j\mu},
\qquad
\Delta E_{\mathcal O}=h(\nu_j-\nu_i),
\]

\[
P_\parallel={M_H\Delta E_{\mathcal O}\over Q_{\mathcal O}}-{Q_{\mathcal O}\over2}.
\]

After the energy delta function and the out-of-plane Maxwellian momentum
are integrated, the remaining atom integral is

\[
\langle |\mathcal M_{\rm COM}|^2\rangle_{\mathcal O}
=
\int {dt\over\sqrt\pi}e^{-t^2}
|\mathcal M_{\rm COM}(P_\parallel,\sqrt{2M_HkT}\,t)|^2.
\]

A tangent map centred on the narrow \(2p\) pole is combined with broad
Gauss-Legendre intervals. Forward and PT-reversed amplitudes are evaluated
independently on the same physical orbit.

With normalized Lebedev weights and normalized Rayleigh phase

\[
\Phi_R(\mu)={3\over4}(1+\mu^2),
\]

the centre-sampled unordered edge conductance is, up to one common scale,

\[
S_{ab}\propto
w_aw_b\Phi_R(\mu_{ab})\,
\nu_i\nu_j(\Delta\nu)^2
e^{-\beta h\nu_j}
S_{\rm MB}(Q,\Delta E)
\langle|\mathcal M_{\rm COM}|^2\rangle.
\]

The dynamic-structure identity
\(S_{\rm MB}(Q,-\Delta E)=e^{-\beta\Delta E}S_{\rm MB}(Q,\Delta E)\)
and amplitude reciprocity make the forward and reverse edge weights equal.
