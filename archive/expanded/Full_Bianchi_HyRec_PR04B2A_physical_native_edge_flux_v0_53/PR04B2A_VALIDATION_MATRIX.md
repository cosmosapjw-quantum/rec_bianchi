output generated by the source's separate cubic interpolation routine. The
observed values are recorded as failures of that *initial diagnostic limit*;
they are not rewritten as passes. They do not affect the exact source-identity
gates, which are byte-level history hashes, nor the physical normalization
identity, which is tested on the exact internally emitted state. No event
measure, conservation, energy, JVP, positivity, or high-precision threshold was
changed after seeing the data.
